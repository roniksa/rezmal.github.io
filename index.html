About

I build technology platforms and AI-driven businesses inside large engineering and infrastructure firms.

As Chief Technology Officer of Michael Baker International, I lead the firm's technology vision: enterprise AI, digital twins, product, data, cloud and information security. Before that I founded and grew the AI and digital-solutions practice at Tetra Tech, taking it from zero to roughly $50M in revenue in about three years. My roots are in research: a PhD in physics with a focus on AI from the University of Alberta, and hands-on machine learning since the mid-2000s.
